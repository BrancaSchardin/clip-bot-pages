clip bot

Short-form video automation

clip-bot is a personal tool that adds animated captions and a title to short video clips and uploads them as drafts to the owner's own TikTok account for review before publishing.

What it does

Transcribes a short clip, renders word-by-word captions and a title, composes a vertical (9:16) video, and uploads it as a draft via the TikTok Content Posting API. The owner always reviews and publishes manually inside the TikTok app.
