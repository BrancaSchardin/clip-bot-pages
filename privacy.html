Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 June 2026

clip-bot ("the app") is a personal automation tool operated by an individual developer. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we access

When you connect your TikTok account, the app uses TikTok's OAuth to obtain an access token limited to the video.upload scope. This token is used solely to upload videos as drafts to your own TikTok account.

Data we store

  • The OAuth access/refresh token, stored locally on the operator's own computer to keep the session active. It is never shared with third parties.
  • The app does not collect, store, or sell personal data of any other TikTok users, and it does not read your TikTok content or profile beyond what is required to upload a draft.

Data we share

None. No data is transmitted to any third party other than TikTok itself for the purpose of uploading your video drafts.

Retention & deletion

Tokens are kept only on the operator's local machine and can be deleted at any time by removing the local token file or revoking access in your TikTok account settings (Security & permissions → Manage app permissions).
